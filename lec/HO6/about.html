About Sunnies Face

Sunnies Face is a Philippine-based beauty brand known for creating high-quality, affordable makeup that's easy to use for everyday looks. Born from the idea that good makeup shouldn't be complicated or expensive, the brand has grown into one of the most recognized names in local beauty, loved for its soft, radiant "no-makeup makeup" aesthetic.

While Sunnies Face also offers skincare and other beauty essentials, this site focuses on our makeup line — the products that started it all.

What We Stand For

  • Affordable, everyday makeup for all
  • Cruelty-free and made with skin-friendly ingredients
  • Inclusive shades for a wide range of skin tones
  • Simple, no-fuss beauty routines

Our Makeup Categories

Face
Our Face collection includes base products like foundation, concealer, contour, bronzer, and powder — designed to create a smooth, natural finish for everyday wear.
Lips
From tints to matte lippies, our Lips collection offers long-lasting color in shades made to complement every skin tone.
Eyes
Our Eyes collection features eyeshadows, brow products, and liners made for creating both subtle and bold looks with ease.
